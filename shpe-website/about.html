About the chapter

SHPE at Southwestern College is a student-run chapter of the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers, open to any student chasing a future in engineering, math, or science. You don't have to be Hispanic to join. You just have to want the community.

Our mission

We exist to change the numbers. Latinos remain underrepresented in engineering fields, and we're here to close that gap one Jaguar at a time, through mentorship, resources, and a community that has your back from your first calculus class to your first job offer.

What we actually do

Weekly meetings, resume and interview workshops, guest speakers from industry, campus tours of engineering programs, community service, and yes, plenty of food. We're a chapter of SHPE National, so members also get access to scholarships, regional conferences, and a huge professional network.
